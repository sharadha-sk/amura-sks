What is the Clean Eating phase?

While you're waiting for your full prescription, you start on the Clean Eating (CE) phase — an anti-inflammatory diet that cuts out known inflammatory foods. Many people with a high inflammatory load find it very difficult to jump straight into a weight-loss phase, so this stage comes first.

Excess water retention can come down to a certain extent during this phase. No weight loss is expected yet — this is about lowering inflammation, not the scale.

Where Clean Eating sits in the bigger Amura protocol

  1. CE — Clean Eating (you are here) — preparatory / anti-inflammatory phase
  2. V1 — Detox phase
  3. V2 — add back dairy and animal products
  4. V3 — add back nuts and seeds
  5. V4 — add back all nightshade vegetables
  6. V5 — add back cereals and fruits
  7. V6 — add back dals, legumes and pulses
  8. Maintenance — hold your target weight long-term

Each of these will get its own tab here once its details are shared — this page is built to grow with you.

What comes after Clean Eating

Once you've procured the recommended nutrients, protein powder, chia seeds and psyllium husk, you'll be ready for the Detox phase (V1) — typically an ~850 kcal/day diet. It sounds intense, but it's designed so you'll actually feel too full to finish a day's chart as your body settles into ketosis. For the first two weeks it's built from vegetables considered safe for most people, plus protein meals, to reduce inflammatory messenger molecules in your system.

After that two-week wash-off, foods get reintroduced one at a time (V2–V6), in escalating order of intolerance risk. If you react to something, you drop it, return to already-tested foods for 2–3 days, then try the next item. Around V3, fasting gets introduced gradually. Once you reach your target weight, Maintenance teaches you how to hold it — while still avoiding your personal intolerant foods to keep inflammation low.

Subscription note: the initial program runs 90 days from day one of Clean Eating, renewable monthly after.

  • Eat within a 12-hour window
  • Dinner should be carbohydrate-free
  • Log your empty-stomach weight every morning
  • On diabetic meds? Log fasting blood sugar every morning

😅 Possible challenges (not everyone gets these)

Carb withdrawal — mild headache, tiredness. Lime juice with salt helps (skip salt if hypertensive).
Irregular bowel movement — usually settles within a couple of days.
Sleep pattern changes — may take longer to fall asleep at first; settles soon.
Light body pain — a tablespoon of ghee can help.

🌱 How to sprout dals at home

Soak overnight, then sprout: white chana, black chana, moong, lobia, masoor, moth bean, rajma, urad — all in whole form with the skin intact. Use them for breakfast or lunch; avoid at dinner since they carry carbs too.
